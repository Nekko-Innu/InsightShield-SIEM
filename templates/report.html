    § 02
    
Methodology & Scope

    
  


  

    Audit Objectives.
    This report evaluates detection coverage and operational follow-through of the Insight Shield SIEM during the reporting period {{ date_from }} → {{ date_to }}. It covers every Wazuh-decoded alert that matched one of the 27 custom detection rules deployed in custom_retail_rules.xml.
  


  

    Scope.
    {{ agents | selectattr('status', 'equalto', 'active') | list | length }} of {{ agents | length }} monitored endpoints were active during the reporting window; remaining endpoints were offline for planned maintenance or were not yet deployed. Alerts were ingested exclusively through the loopback-restricted webhook at /wazuh-webhook.
  


  

    Methodology.
    Each Wazuh alert was decoded, enriched with an AI-generated plain-language translation (Groq → Gemini → OpenRouter → static-template fallback chain), and validated by validator.py against a structure, word-count, and forbidden-term filter. Valid translations were stored in SQLite (WAL mode) with a PHT timestamp. Severity routing followed the matrix in alert_router.py, including after-hours escalation (business window 08:00–21:00 PHT). The daily security posture score was calculated by health_score.py and reset at midnight PHT.
  


  

    Limitations.
    This audit is scoped exclusively to the NIST CSF 2.0 — Detect function; Identify, Protect, Respond, Recover, and Govern are out of scope per the project thesis. The report includes only events that matched at least one custom rule; endpoint-native events that did not trigger a rule are excluded. AI-generated summaries are cached for at most one hour; cold-cache generation may add 2–4 seconds of latency to the first request.{% if current_user.is_it_admin %} Raw Wazuh JSON payloads in Appendix A preserve the original decoder output for independent verification.{% endif %}
  


  

    Framework Alignment. Aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0:
  

  

    Identify — out of scope
    Protect — out of scope
    Detect · in scope
    Respond — out of scope
    Recover — out of scope
    Govern — out of scope
