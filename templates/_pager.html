{# templates/_pager.html Prev/Next pager for the IT alerts table. Caller must provide `pager` (built in web/blueprints/dashboards.py's dashboard_it): page, last_page, total, per_page, first_row, last_row. Links are built with the url_with() Jinja global so paging keeps whatever filters are already applied. No numbered page links: with a Next that carries the filters and a table that is read newest-first, jumping to page 9 is not a thing anyone does — they narrow the filter instead. Included AFTER and outside the .table-scroll wrapper, which is capped at 420px; a pager inside it would scroll away from the user. #}
